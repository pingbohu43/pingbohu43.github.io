About

Brief profile

Hello! I am currently a postdoctoral research scientist in biostatistics at Columbia University Irving Medical Center, working with Prof. Yifei Sun and Prof. Yize Zhao (Yale University). Prior to this, I was a research trainee at University of Western Ontario and Sichuan University. My research interests focus on statistical methodology and applications to address challenges concerning time-to-event data, longitudinal data, noisy data, survival analysis, nonparametric statistics, causal inference, and statistical learning. Please feel free to reach out if you’re interested in discussing research and potential collaboration opportunities!
