About

Brief profile

Hello! I am currently a postdoctoral research scientist in biostatistics at Columbia University Irving Medical Center, co-supervised by Prof. Yifei Sun and Prof. Yize Zhao (Yale University). Prior to this, I was a research trainee at University of Western Ontario and Sichuan University. My research interests focus on statistical methodology and applications to address challenges concerning time-to-event data, longitudinal data, noisy data, survival analysis, nonparametric statistics, causal inference, and statistical learning.
